Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This policy describes how the Shakyfinity desktop companion handles information when you use it to connect TikTok LIVE gift events with Minecraft actions.

Information Shakyfinity handles

  • TikTok authorization: When you choose to sign in, TikTok Login Kit returns authorization tokens and an account identifier. The app keeps these in memory while it is running and clears them when you log out or close the app.
  • LIVE event data: The app receives public event details such as the sender name, gift name, gift count, and diamond value from the TikTok LIVE stream you connect to. These are used to display events and trigger the interactions you configure.
  • Minecraft connection details: The ServerTap address and key are used to check and maintain a connection to the Minecraft server you choose. The key is held in memory while the app runs.
  • Interaction settings: Your interaction names, triggers, and commands are stored in browser storage on your device.

How information is used

Shakyfinity uses this information only to authenticate your TikTok account, receive LIVE events, connect to your configured Minecraft server, and run the commands you choose. The current desktop app does not send these settings to a Shakyfinity cloud service.

Third-party services

Signing in is handled by TikTok Login Kit and is subject to TikTok's own policies. LIVE event reading currently relies on an unofficial third-party connector that communicates with TikTok's public LIVE event service; its behavior and availability may change. Minecraft communication is sent to the ServerTap endpoint you configure. These services process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Website hosting

If this policy is hosted with GitHub Pages, GitHub may process standard technical access logs, including IP addresses, for security purposes under GitHub's privacy practices.

Retention and choices

OAuth tokens and ServerTap keys are kept in app memory for the current session. Log out of TikTok or close Shakyfinity to clear them. Interaction settings remain in local browser storage until you remove them or clear that browser's site data.

Children and contact

Shakyfinity is intended for creators who can use TikTok and Minecraft under the applicable platform rules. For privacy questions about Shakyfinity, contact the project owner through the support channel listed on the official Shakyfinity website.

This draft describes the current local app design. Update it if the app begins storing information on a server, adds analytics, or changes how integrations work.
